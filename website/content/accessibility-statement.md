We want Tallybook, and this website, to work for everyone, including people who
use a keyboard, a screen reader, zoom or high-contrast settings.

## What we aim for

We aim to meet the [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/)
at level AA. In practice that means:

- Everything can be reached and used with a keyboard, with a visible focus ring.
- Text and controls have sufficient color contrast, in light and dark mode.
- Color is never the only way information is shown; numbers and labels carry it too.
- Images have text alternatives, and pages use proper headings and landmarks.
- The app follows your system's light or dark setting and text size.

## Known gaps

Tallybook is young, and some screens, charts especially, are still being
improved for screen readers. If something gets in your way, we'd like to fix it.
