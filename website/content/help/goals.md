**Goals** are things you're saving toward. They add up month after month
instead of starting over, and Tallybook tells you when you'll get there at
the rate you're going.

## Set a goal

Click **New goal** (or **Set a goal**).

{{< shot "new-goal" "The New goal window" >}}

1. **What are you saving for?** A trip, a cushion for slow months, a new car.
2. **How much** you want to save.
3. **By when**, if there's a date. Leave it blank for someday.
4. Which account it's **Kept in**.
5. What you've **Already saved**, if you're not starting from zero.
6. Click **Save goal**.

Saving shows up in the **Putting money aside** part of your
[budget score](/help/this-month/).
