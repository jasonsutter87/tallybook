**Goals** are things you're saving toward. They add up month after month
instead of starting over. Set a target, and Tallybook tells you when you'll get
there at the rate you're going.

## Set a goal

1. Click **New goal**.
2. **What are you saving for?** Give it a name.
3. Enter **How much** you need.
4. Optionally set **By when**. Leave it blank for someday.
5. Choose the account it's **Kept in**.
6. If you've already put some aside, enter it under **Already saved**.
7. Click **Save goal**.
