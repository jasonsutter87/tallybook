**This month** is the household budget: what came in, what went out, and
whether you're ahead. Use the arrows next to the month to look back or ahead.

{{< shot "month" "This month: came in, went out and ahead by, the plan, who it was for, where it went, and the budget score" >}}

## Make a plan

A plan sets a limit for the categories, or people, you want to keep an eye on.
You don't need a line for everything.

1. Click **Make a plan**.
2. Under **What there is to work with this month**, enter what you expect to
   bring in, or leave it blank if you'd rather not say.
3. Add **Budget lines**: pick a **Category** or a **Person**, and set a limit.
4. Click **Save plan**.

{{< shot "make-a-plan" "The plan for the month: what there is to work with, and budget lines by category or person" >}}

Each month starts with the plan from the month before. Changes apply from the
month you're on.

## Log money

**Log money** is for cash and anything your bank won't send over.

1. Choose **Money out**, **Money in** or **Move money**.
2. Enter **How much?** and **What was it?**
3. Choose the account it was **Paid from** and **When**.
4. **Who was it for?** Leave it as shared for the whole household, or pick a
   person.
5. Add a note if you like, and click **Log it**.

## Who the money is for

Add the people in your household under **People**, then tag spending with a
person to see, and budget, what each of you costs. Untagged spending counts as
shared.

## Budget score

The **Budget score** sums up how the month is going, out of five parts:

| Part | Points |
|---|---|
| Living within your income | 35 |
| Bills paid on time | 25 |
| Spending to plan | 20 |
| Putting money aside | 10 |
| Steady income | 10 |

Each part says why it scored what it did, like "no plan for this month" or
"not enough history yet".
