**This month** is the household's home base. It shows the month so far:
what **Came in**, what **Went out**, and whether you're ahead. Use the arrows
next to the month's name to look back.

{{< shot "month" "This month: money in and out, a prompt to make a plan, who it was for, where it went and the budget score" >}}

## Make a plan

Click **Make a plan** to set limits for the categories, or people, you want
to keep an eye on.

{{< shot "make-a-plan" "The Plan for this month window" >}}

1. Optionally enter **What there is to work with this month**, usually what
   you expect to bring in. Leave it blank if you'd rather not say.
2. Add budget lines by **Category** or by **Person**.
3. Click **Save plan**.

Each month starts with the plan from the month before, and changes apply from
this month on.

## Who it was for

Click **People** (or **Add people**) to add the people in your household.

{{< shot "people" "The Who the money is for window" >}}

Tag spending with a person to see, and budget, what each of you costs.
Untagged spending counts as shared.

## Budget score

The **Budget score** sums up how the household is doing, with five parts:
**Living within your income**, **Bills paid on time**, **Spending to plan**,
**Putting money aside** and **Steady income**. Each shows its points and why,
so you can see what to work on.
