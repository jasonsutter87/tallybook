Everything in Tallybook lives in a **book**: one file on your computer that
holds your accounts, transactions, invoices, budget and reports. There's no
account to sign up for and no Tallybook cloud.

{{< shot "welcome" "The welcome screen, with Create a new book, Open a book and your recent books" >}}

## Create a book

1. On the welcome screen, click **Create a new book**.
2. Choose what the book is for:
   - **A business**: invoices, bills, reports and tax prep.
   - **A household**: a monthly budget, bills coming up, and goals.
   - **Both, in one book**: for the self-employed. Each side keeps its own
     numbers, and money you pay yourself moves between them.
3. Type a name. The window shows where the file will be saved.
4. Click **Create book**.

{{< shot "create-book" "Create a book: a business, a household, or both in one book" >}}

Picked one side and want the other later? Add it in
[Settings → Business & household](/help/settings/#business-and-household).

## Open a book

- **Recent** on the welcome screen lists the books you've opened. Click one
  to open it.
- **Open a book** finds a book file anywhere else, including a backup.
- **Switch book**, at the bottom of the sidebar, closes the book you're in and
  goes back to the welcome screen.

## Find your way around

The sidebar groups everything by what it's for:

| Section | What's there |
|---|---|
| Top | **Home**, **Review** (with a count of transactions waiting) and **Accounts** |
| Household | **This month**, **Bills & due dates** and **Goals** |
| Business | **Invoices**, **Vendor bills** and **Customers & vendors** |
| Insights & setup | **Reports**, **Taxes**, **Bank connections**, **Rules** and **Settings** |

Next, bring in your transactions: [connect your bank or import a
statement](/help/bank-connections/).
