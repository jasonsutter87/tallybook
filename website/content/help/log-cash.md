Most money comes in through [bank sync or an imported
statement](/help/bank-connections/). For everything else there are two ways
to record it yourself.

## Log cash

**Log cash**, on Home, is for the till, cash on hand, checks, and anything
the bank feed won't see.

1. Choose what happened: **Sale or money in**, **Expense**, or **Deposit or
   move**.
2. Enter **How much?** and **What was it?** (the category).
3. Choose the account it was **Paid from** (or paid into) and **When**.
4. Optionally choose who it was **Paid to**. Cash paid to a contractor still
   counts toward their 1099.
5. Add a **Note** if you like, and click **Log it**.

## Record a transaction or journal entry

**Accounts → New journal entry** is for things that didn't come through a
bank feed, invoice or bill.

- **Simple** asks for a date, description and amount, and which account the
  money moved **from** and **to**. For example, paying rent moves money from
  Checking to Rent; paying off a card moves it from Checking to Visa.
- **Journal entry** is full double-entry: a line per account with a debit or
  credit and a line memo. Debits must equal credits. Use it for adjustments
  your accountant asks for.

## Accounts

**Accounts** lists every place money lives, comes from or goes to, with
switches for the **Household** and **Business** sides. The cards at the top
total your **Cash & bank**, **Cards & loans** and **Net worth**.

**Add account** adds one. Choose which side of the book it's on
(**Business** or **Household**), give it a name, and pick what kind it is:
bank, credit card, loan, asset, equity, income or expense. A bank account
can start with an **Opening balance** as of a date. The **account number** is
optional, a short code like 1020 to sort your chart.

Click an account to open it.
