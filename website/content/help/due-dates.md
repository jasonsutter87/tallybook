**Bills & due dates** is for the home's regular bills: rent, the phone,
insurance and the rest. Once they're in, you see what's due today and this
week at a glance. Bills tick themselves off when the payment shows up from
your bank.

## Add a bill

Click **Add a bill**.

{{< shot "add-household-bill" "The Add a bill window for a household bill" >}}

1. Give it a **Name** and an **Amount**. Leave the amount blank if you don't
   know it yet.
2. Pick a **Category**, **How often** (*Every month*, *Every few months* or
   *Every week*) and the **Due on day**.
3. Optionally choose what it's **Paid from** and **Who it's for**.
4. **Bank description contains** helps tell this bill apart from others in
   the same category, for example *VERIZON*.
5. Tick **Changes month to month** for bills like power and water, and
   **Pays itself (auto-pay)** for ones on auto-pay.
6. Click **Add bill**.

Business bills from vendors go in [Vendor bills](/help/bills/) instead.
