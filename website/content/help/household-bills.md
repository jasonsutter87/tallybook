**Bills & due dates** keeps the rent, the phone, insurance and the rest in one
place, so you can see what's due today and this week. A bill ticks itself off
when its payment shows up from your bank.

(Bills your business owes vendors are in [Vendor bills](/help/bills/).)

## Add a bill

1. Click **Add a bill**.
2. Enter a **Name** and the **Amount**. Leave the amount blank if you don't
   know it yet.
3. Choose its **Category**.
4. Choose **How often** (*Every month*, *Every few months* or *Every week*)
   and the day it's due.
5. Optionally choose the account it's **Paid from** and **Who it's for**.
6. **Bank description contains** helps tell this bill apart from others in
   the same category, like VERIZON.
7. Turn on **Changes month to month** for bills like power and water, and
   **Pays itself** for auto-pay.
8. Click **Add bill**.
