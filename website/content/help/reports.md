**Reports** turns your books into five standard reports, each in plain
English. Every report has **Print / PDF**, and most can switch between the
**Business** and **Household** side.

| Report | Answers |
|---|---|
| **Profit & loss** | Did you make money? Income minus expenses for a period. |
| **Balance sheet** | What you own and what you owe, on a given day. |
| **Who owes you** | Unpaid customer invoices, grouped by how late they are (A/R aging). |
| **Who you owe** | Unpaid bills to vendors, grouped by how late they are (A/P aging). |
| **Trial balance** | Every account's debit or credit balance, for your accountant. |

## Profit & loss

{{< shot "profit-loss" "The Profit and loss report with net profit, income, expenses and where the money went" >}}

Pick a period: **This month**, **Last month**, **This quarter**, **Year to
date**, **Last year**, or **Custom** dates. The top shows **Net profit** and
the share of income you kept. Below are income and expenses by account, and
**Where the money went** ranks your spending categories.

## Balance sheet and trial balance

Both are **As of** a date: **Today**, **End of last month** or **End of last
year**. The balance sheet groups accounts into what you own, what you owe and
what's yours (equity), and shows **Balanced ✓** when everything adds up.

## Who owes you, and who you owe

{{< shot "who-owes-you" "The Who owes you report showing totals by age" >}}

These aging reports split balances into **Current**, **1–30**, **31–60**,
**61–90** and **90+** days, per customer or vendor. Past-due amounts are
called out at the top: *worth a friendly nudge*.
