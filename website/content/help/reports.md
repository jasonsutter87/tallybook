**Reports** has five, each for the **Business** or **Household** side. Every
report has **Print / PDF**.

| Report | Answers |
|---|---|
| **Profit & loss** | Did you make money? Income minus expenses for a period. |
| **Balance sheet** | What you own and what you owe, on a given day. |
| **Who owes you** | Unpaid customer invoices, grouped by how late they are (A/R aging). |
| **Who you owe** | Unpaid bills to vendors, grouped by how late they are (A/P aging). |
| **Trial balance** | Every account's debit or credit balance, for your accountant. |

## Profit & loss

Pick a period: **This month**, **Last month**, **This quarter**, **Year to
date**, **Last year**, or **Custom** dates. The top shows net profit and the
share of income you kept. Below are income and expenses by account, each with
its share of the total, and a chart of **where the money went**.

## Balance sheet

Choose the day it's **As of**: **Today**, **End of last month** or **End of
last year**. It lists what you own, what you owe, and what's yours (equity),
and shows **Balanced ✓** when the two sides agree.

{{< shot "balance-sheet" "Balance sheet: assets, liabilities and equity, balanced" >}}

## Who owes you and who you owe

Both group open balances by age: **Current**, **1–30**, **31–60**, **61–90**
and **90+** days late, one row per customer or vendor. The top shows the
total, how much isn't due yet, and how much is past due.

{{< shot "who-owes-you" "Who owes you: open customer balances by how late they are" >}}

## Trial balance

Every account's debit or credit balance as of a day, the way accountants like
to check the books.

"Year to date" counts from the first month of your fiscal year, set in
[Settings → Fiscal year](/help/settings/). For the tax worksheets, see
[Tax prep](/help/taxes/).
