**Accounts** lists every place money lives, comes from, or goes to: your
bank and card accounts, and the income and spending categories behind your
reports. Switch between **Household** and **Business** at the top.

Totals across the top show **Cash & bank**, **Cards & loans** and your
**Net worth** (what you own minus what you owe). Below, accounts are grouped:
bank and cash, starting balances, income and spending. Spending categories
are tagged **Fixed**, **Moves with use** or **Your choice**, which helps
when you plan a budget.

* **Add a bank account** adds a checking or savings account so you can start
  tracking cash. To have its transactions flow in on their own, see
  [Connecting your bank](/help/bank-connections/).

## Record a transaction by hand

For anything that didn't come through a bank feed, invoice or bill, click
**New journal entry**.

{{< shot "journal-entry" "The Record a transaction window in Simple mode" >}}

In **Simple** mode, fill in the **Date**, **Description** and **Amount**,
then where the money came **From** and went **To**. Money moves from → to:
paid rent is from Checking to Rent, and paying off a card is from Checking to
Visa. Switch to **Journal entry** for a full debit-and-credit entry. Click
**Save transaction**.

For cash and checks, the quicker way is **Log cash** on the
[Home screen](/help/home/).
