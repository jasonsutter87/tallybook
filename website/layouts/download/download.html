Opening it the first time

Tallybook isn't signed by Apple or Microsoft yet, so your computer asks before opening it once.

On a Mac

Right-click Tallybook in Applications, choose Open, then Open again. If it says the app is damaged, run xattr -cr /Applications/Tallybook.app in Terminal and try again.

On Windows

If you see "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.

Then, in a few minutes

  1. Make a book for your household, your business, or both.
  2. Bring in transactions. Import a CSV or OFX file from your bank's website for free, or connect your bank with a Personal plan and sign in on your bank's own page.
  3. Review and plan. Accept Tallybook's suggestions, set this month's plan, and add the bills it spotted.
